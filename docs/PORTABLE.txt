Portable mode
=============

Everything lives next to the executable. No install, no registry.

1. Copy crowman_study.exe anywhere writable.
2. Rename config.sample.ini to config.ini beside the exe.
3. Run it.

An adjacent config.ini wins over the AppData copy. Secrets are still DPAPI
sealed, so a portable folder copied to another profile needs the key
re-entered. History and logs still go to %APPDATA% and %LOCALAPPDATA%.
